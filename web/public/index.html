Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to the questions that matter before you paste a link.

주소는 어디에 붙여넣으면 되나요?

페이지 맨 위의 큰 URL 입력칸에 붙여넣으면 됩니다. 플랫폼을 고를 필요 없이 주소만 넣고 Analyze link를 누르면 자동 감지됩니다.

Do I have to choose YouTube, Instagram, Threads, Douyin or Xiaohongshu first?

No. The URL is detected automatically. The platform labels under the input are status indicators, not tabs you need to navigate.

Why can a public link still fail?

The post may have been deleted, region-restricted, login-gated, rate-limited, or changed by the source platform. Short share links can also expire. AVOCADOSS does not bypass access controls.

Does the site keep a permanent history of my downloaded media?

No permanent media library is part of the downloader. Files generated for delivery are temporary and are removed after the response lifecycle.

Can I use the downloader for copyrighted media?

Use it only where you own the media, have permission or a license, or otherwise have a lawful right to save it. Source-platform names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Is clicking an advertisement required to download?

No. Advertising, when enabled, is separate from the download action. AVOCADOSS does not require an ad click to unlock a file.
