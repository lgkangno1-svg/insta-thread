Frequently asked questions

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, Instagram Reels, Xiaohongshu, Threads and Douyin public links are the target platforms.

Can I choose a thumbnail instead of the video?

Instagram Reels, Threads and Douyin expose selectable thumbnail or cover candidates when the source provides them.

Are private posts supported?

No. The service is intended for publicly accessible media and does not bypass private-account controls or DRM.

Why can a working link suddenly fail?

Social platforms change page structures, tokens and anti-bot systems frequently. Extractors are updated as upstream behavior changes.

Do you store my downloads?

The intended production design uses temporary files only when required, followed by automatic deletion after delivery.
