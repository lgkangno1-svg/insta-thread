Social media downloader FAQ

These answers cover the public-media workflow shared by the YouTube, Instagram, Threads, Douyin and Xiaohongshu downloaders.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, Instagram Reels and supported posts, public Threads posts, public Douyin videos, and public Xiaohongshu video or image notes are the target platforms.

Can I choose a thumbnail instead of the video?

Instagram Reels, Threads and Douyin expose selectable thumbnail or cover candidates when the public source provides them. Xiaohongshu image notes can expose individual images and a ZIP option.

Are private posts supported?

No. The service is intended for publicly accessible media and does not bypass private-account controls, login gates, paywalls or DRM.

Why can a working link suddenly fail?

Social platforms change page structures, share-link redirects, tokens and anti-abuse systems frequently. A deleted or restricted source can also become unavailable independently of AVOCADOSS.

What should I try when a share link fails?

First copy a fresh share URL from the original public post. If a short link has expired, the new link often resolves correctly. If the source platform itself requires authentication, AVOCADOSS will not bypass it.

Do you store my downloads?

No permanent user media library is provided. Files that require server-side preparation are temporary and are removed after delivery.

Where should I paste the link?

Use the large URL field at the top of the home page or any platform guide. Platform selection is automatic.
