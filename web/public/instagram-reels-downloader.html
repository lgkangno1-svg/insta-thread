Why Instagram links sometimes fail

Private or login-gated media

AVOCADOSS does not authenticate into private accounts or bypass Instagram access restrictions.

Platform API changes

Instagram changes anonymous media endpoints and anti-abuse behavior. The adapter is maintained independently from the platform.

Removed or unavailable post

A deleted, region-limited or otherwise unavailable source cannot be recovered by the downloader.

Instagram downloader FAQ

Do I need to log in with my Instagram account?

No account sign-in is requested by AVOCADOSS for the public downloader. If Instagram itself requires login to expose the media, the tool does not bypass that restriction.

Can I save the Reel cover or thumbnail?

When the public Instagram response provides image candidates, the result includes selectable thumbnail entries. The exact number and resolution vary by post.

Does this support Instagram Stories?

The current public downloader does not advertise Story access. Stories frequently depend on account/session access, so AVOCADOSS does not claim support that has not been verified.

Why can a link work today and fail later?

The source platform can change endpoints, expire media URLs, add rate limits or require authentication. Public extraction tools must adapt to those upstream changes.
