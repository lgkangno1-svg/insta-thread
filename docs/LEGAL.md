# Legal and policy launch checklist

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice.

- Process only publicly accessible, non-DRM media.
- Do not implement private-account bypass, credential theft, DRM circumvention, or access-control evasion.
- State that users must have rights/permission to download and reuse media.
- Publish Terms, Privacy, and Copyright/DMCA contact pages.
- Provide a rights-holder/takedown contact channel.
- Do not permanently host downloaded media.
- Keep operational logs minimal and avoid logging sensitive query strings/cookies.
- Do not accept user browser-session cookies in the public MVP unless a secure, explicit design is later approved.
- Review each platform's current terms before public launch and whenever extractor behavior changes.
